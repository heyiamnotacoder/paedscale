# British Journal of Clinical Pharmacology: Original Article requirements

Read from the BJCP author guidelines on 2026-11-27:
https://bpspubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/13652125/author-guidelines.html
Check the page again before submission, because the guidelines change.

- **Length:** about 4,000 words. Going well over needs a justification at submission.
- **Abstract:** at most 250 words, structured as Aim, Methods, Results, Conclusion.
- **Title page:**
  - Title: at most 150 characters including spaces, and no abbreviations.
  - Full author names and affiliations.
  - Principal Investigator statement. Without one, say there is no Principal Investigator.
  - Keywords.
  - Word count, table count and figure count.
- **Boxes:**
  - "What is already known about this subject": up to 3 bullets, at most 50 words.
  - "What this study adds": up to 3 bullets, at most 50 words.
- **Sections:** Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion.
- **Figures and tables:** preferably at most 8 in total. Anything more goes to the supporting information.
- **References:** no limit. Free-format submission is allowed, and the journal converts references to AMA style at publication.
- **Nomenclature:**
  - Follow the BPS/IUPHAR Guide to Pharmacology.
  - Link key ligands and targets to the Guide to Pharmacology at first mention.
  - Supply a list of the linked terms.
- **Statistics:** name measures of spread (SD, SEM, CI) in table headings.
- **Reporting guidelines:** encouraged where one applies. No EQUATOR guideline fits a model-evaluation benchmark exactly. Cite TRIPOD where it applies to prediction performance.
- **Statements:**
  - Data availability (required).
  - Conflicts of interest (required, even if none).
  - Funding, in the Acknowledgements.
- **AI tools:**
  - Use is allowed, but an AI tool cannot be an author.
  - Describe the use in detail in the Methods or Acknowledgements.
  - The authors are accountable for all AI-produced content.
- **Code and data:** cite the public repository that holds the scripts and data.
- **Preprints:** allowed at any time.
